Competitive intelligence has an attention problem

04.10.2026 · 1 MIN READ

Competitive intelligence in B2B SaaS has never had more information.

CRM data. Gong calls. Competitor updates. Win/loss. Market signals. AI summaries.

Access is no longer the problem.
Attention is.

Modern CI is becoming less about collecting information and more about deciding what deserves attention.

I see three roles emerging:

Curator Separates signal from noise.
Interpreter Connects signals, adds context and explains what matters.
Architect Designs how intelligence is captured, connected and accessed.

Tools like Salesforce, Gong, Klue, Glean and Copilot can create a connected intelligence layer. AI makes that intelligence easier to retrieve and interrogate.

But access to more information does not create better intelligence.

Someone still has to decide what matters.

That may be where the real value of CI increasingly sits.
