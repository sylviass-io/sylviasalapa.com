Thinking

Where intelligence is going.

01

Intelligence should be continuous

Markets don't update quarterly. Competitive intelligence shouldn't either. The most useful systems detect change continuously and surface what matters when it matters.

02

AI changes the workflow, not the judgement

Discovery, classification and synthesis can increasingly be automated. Deciding what matters, what it means and what to do about it remains the interesting part.

03

Another repository isn't the answer

Intelligence creates value when market signals, competitive knowledge and commercial data connect — and when people can retrieve what they need without knowing where it lives.

04

The analyst is moving upstream

Finding information is becoming cheaper. Asking the right question, challenging assumptions and translating evidence into implications are becoming more valuable.
