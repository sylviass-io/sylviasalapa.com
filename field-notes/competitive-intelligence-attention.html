Competitive intelligence
has an attention
problem.

Competitive intelligence has never had more information.

CRM data. Gong calls. Competitor updates. Win/loss. Market signals. AI summaries.

Access is no longer the problem.
Attention is.
The role is shifting with it.
Curator
Separates signal from noise.
Interpreter
Connects signals and explains what matters.
Architect
Designs how intelligence is captured, connected and accessed.
AI makes intelligence easier to retrieve.

Someone still has to decide what matters.
