Competitive intelligence has an attention problem

Competitive intelligence has never had more information.

CRM data. Gong calls. Competitor updates. Win/loss. Market signals. AI summaries.

Access is no longer the problem. Attention is.

I see modern CI increasingly playing three roles:

Curator: separates signal from noise.

Interpreter: connects signals and explains what matters.

Architect: designs how intelligence is captured, connected and accessed.

AI makes intelligence easier to retrieve.

Someone still has to decide what matters.

That may be where the real value of CI sits.
